How scoring works

Winning-team checklist: the things most tournament teams with a 70%+ win rate have (61 of 377 teams), such as speed control, spread damage, lead support (Fake Out, Intimidate or redirection), a Mega, no major weak spot and pressure on the top 30. A missing check is a gap to plan around, not a verdict. Move sets for other players' teams are estimated from popular sets, so a move only some players run counts as "partly" (~).

Defense: for each attacking type, Pokémon weak to it are matched against Pokémon that resist it, and immunities count as resists. Abilities counted on defense: Levitate, Eelevate, Earth Eater, Flash Fire, Well-Baked Body, Water Absorb, Storm Drain, Dry Skin, Volt Absorb, Lightning Rod, Motor Drive, Sap Sipper, Thick Fat, Heatproof, Water Bubble, Purifying Salt, Fluffy.

Offense: each Pokémon's damaging moves, after Aerilate, Pixilate, Refrigerate, Galvanize, Dragonize, Liquid Voice, Scrappy and Tinted Lens change how they hit.

Plans: Pokémon are rated from in-game rankings, tournament use and ladder usage, then checked for speed control, Fake Out or redirection, weather and Trick Room fit, and how often real teams pair them with yours. Fun mode rates them by power and fit instead of popularity.

NastyPlot rating (★): a 0–100 score for every legal Pokémon. It blends seven sources: win rates at 443 Limitless tournaments and 6 official VGC events (Pokémon Zone), corrected for small samples and for mirror matches; each Pokémon's own share of team wins from 10,083 Limitless teams (fit jointly so a Pokémon isn't credited for its partners' wins, which predicted later tournament results better than raw win rates); plus popularity among strong players (the official in-game Ranked Doubles ranking via op.gg, Smogon 1760+ usage, the top 10% of Showdown replays via project-red-data on GitHub, Showdown Tier). The Ratings tab shows every Pokémon on a tier board and a usage-vs-rating map. The weights were tuned on held-out data: ladder win rates did not predict tournament results, so they are shown but not scored. Popularity counts for every Pokémon in full (it's measured on 10,000+ teams); only win rates are shrunk when a Pokémon has few games. In-game and ladder rankings list species, so their numbers are split between a species' regular and Mega forms by how strong players split them. Each rating sheet also shows three outside tier lists (Pokémon Zone, PokeSynergy, PokeTools) for comparison. Stats, typing against the top 30 and tools (abilities, support moves) fill in where there is little data. Hidden gems are rarely used Pokémon that rate 55+ and whose results hold up. Suggestions from the whole roster need a rating of 50+.

Archetypes: each team is read by its speed mode (Tailwind, Trick Room, both or none), its field (sun, rain, sand, snow, and Grassy, Psychic, Electric or Misty Terrain) and control plans like Perish Trap. The Matchup tab compares both archetypes and weighs them when it picks your 4 and your lead.

Roster, abilities, moves and learnsets come from Pokémon Showdown's Champions data.
