How we work

One step at a time, in this order. One site change and one grade change in progress at most.

Sealed test set. Recent tournaments we never tune on; used only to confirm each phase.

A change is kept only if it clearly beats today on the sealed set, by more than normal chance variation. Otherwise the simpler version stays.

Honest targets. Picking match winners from teams alone tops out in the high 50s%. We aim for not worse, well calibrated (60% means 60%), and letters that clearly differ.
