DAMAGE CALC — SIM'S PICK: "THEIR SIDE" (a card-table duel + your KO Board). Design brief, no code.

Sim picked this from mock-ups made in a separate chat. The six attached pictures are the
reference. Two examples (Sneasler's Close Combat into Kingambit; Mega Charizard Y's Heat
Wave into Archaludon), each as: desktop 1440 x 900, the full phone page at 390 wide, and
the phone's first screen at 390 x 844 with the bottom bar. All in dark and light.
They were drawn over dist/v2.html with real numbers from today's calc and your KO Board
recipe, but NOT by your rules (literal colours, off-scale sizes, off-grid spacing, a 3D
tilt on desktop, filters). Every number below is a target to translate. Match the pictures
first, the numbers second. Nothing goes to Preview or live without Sim's go.
Phone and desktop are two different layouts of the same idea. iPad is NOT drawn (see 8).

THE IDEA
The calc is a card duel. Both Pokémon are the info sheet's Card. The attacker's moves are a
hand of move cards; the one in play lies on the table. Weather and terrain are field cards;
the five conditions are tokens. The controls ARE the scene: there is no form under it.
Your KO Board is "their side": on desktop it is laid out on the table around the defender;
on a phone it is its own section below the table.

0. WHAT STAYS (all of today's Damage Calc; same data, same wording unless listed in 10)
Attacker and defender pickers with "nature, ability" and the assumed / estimated spread
tag; the attacker's set moves and "Other moves"; Swap; weather, terrain and the five
conditions; the result (range, KO text, "x–y of z HP", effectiveness, every note, "Not
counted", "Fewest points to always survive", the two-line set readout); Report a mistake;
How the calc works; both set-details folds; the best-hits table in both directions.

1. THE TWO POKÉMON CARDS (both sizes)
The info sheet's Card: cut corners, the type-to-Pokémon-colour edge, glow, giant outlined
name letters behind the Pokémon. On it: the role (ATTACKER / DEFENDER) top-left, the
Pokémon, the name with its picker arrow, "nature, ability", and the spread tag. A Mega shows
a small blue "Mega" on its own line above the name. Keep clear space between the Pokémon's
feet and its name.

2. MOVE CARDS (both sizes)
Each move card is a small version of the Card:
- cut corners; a thin edge running type colour, white, type colour, darker type colour;
- a dark face with a type-colour glow in the top-right corner (light mode: white running
  to a pale tint of the type colour);
- a type pattern in that corner. The mock-up has four, shared across the 18 types:
  burst (Fighting, Fire, Electric, Dragon), drops (Poison, Water, Grass, Bug, Fairy),
  rings (Normal, Psychic, Ghost, Flying), shards (Ice, Rock, Steel, Ground, Dark);
- the move's power as giant outlined numbers in the top corner, behind the text;
- top to bottom: a slanted type chip, the move name in the logo font, a physical or special
  mark with "Physical · 120 power" (desktop only), the move's own damage range, its own KO
  text, and a thin slanted bar of how much HP it takes.
- The card IN PLAY is landscape: type chip and name on the left, range and KO text on the
  right, the bar along the bottom, one soft sheen, a glow in its type colour.
- A move with NO EFFECT: face greyed, one thin line struck across it, "No effect", and the
  calc's own reason ("Kingambit is immune to Poison.").
- "Other moves": a face-down card with a fine crossed-line back, an edge in the logo's blue
  and pink, the words "Other moves" and a large outlined "+" (desktop adds "Every damaging
  move it learns").

3. FIELD CARDS AND TOKENS (both sizes)
- Weather and Terrain are cut-corner field cards showing the label, the current value in the
  logo font and five small slanted pips with the current one lit. A set weather is a lit
  card (Sun is gold); unset is a dashed empty slot reading "None".
- The five conditions are tokens: outline when off, filled blue when on.

4. DESKTOP (reference: pictures 1 and 3)
- RESULT BAND above the table, not tilted, one centred row: the range as the big slanted
  number (about 84), then a small column beside it: the move line ("CLOSE COMBAT · SUN",
  plus "· HITS BOTH FOES" when it applies), the KO text on a slanted plate in the move's
  type colour, a slanted damage bar (solid to the low roll, hatched to the high roll), and
  the "194–230 of 207 HP · Fighting is super effective twice over" line.
- THE TABLE: a surface about 1120 x 660, tilted away about 27 degrees, faint grid, thin
  edge, large rounded corners. Result plus table are about 750 tall: first screen.
- FAR ROW (their side): the defender's card in the centre (about 200 x 280). The KO Board
  as two wings of small cut-corner tiles, 5 across x 3 down on each side of that card
  (about 66 x 80, gap 6), surest KO first. Each tile: the Pokémon, the small picture of
  which of yours hits it (top-left), the HP-left bar, the slanted "KO" stamp. Tiles that go
  down in one hit are tinted and dimmed. The Pokémon on the table is outlined in blue.
  A caption over each wing carries your counts: "THE KO BOARD · 18 DOWN IN ONE HIT" (left),
  "8 CAN GO DOWN · 4 SURVIVE" (right). Tapping a tile puts it on the table as the defender.
- MIDDLE ROW: Weather card (left) and Terrain card (right), about 176 x 104; the card in
  play in the centre (about 360 x 116, lying at about -3 degrees); Swap, a small pill
  between the Weather card and the card in play.
- NEAR ROW (your side): the attacker's card (about 204 x 220) on the left; the hand, upright
  move cards about 190 x 216, fanned (about -7, 0, +7 degrees, overlapping about 16) with
  "Other moves" at the end; the five tokens (round, about 72) at the right.

- RESULT BLOCK (about 120 tall):

  row 4: the effectiveness line, when there is one.
- THE TABLE (full width less a small margin, about 540 tall, faint grid, rounded corners):

  e) the hand at the bottom: up to three move cards plus "Other moves", about 98 x 134 each,

- THE KO BOARD is its own section BELOW the table (Sim: on a phone it must not be on the
  table). Heading "The KO Board"; your caption; then three groups, each under its count as a

- Then Details and the two set folds.

6. UNDER THE SCENE (both sizes)
Details (notes, "Fewest points to always survive", the set readout, Report a mistake, and on

shows the current values ("32 Attack points · Adamant · White Herb · Unburden · stat stage 0").

7. KNOWN PROBLEMS IN THE MOCK-UP (please solve, do not copy)
- Desktop: the far end of the tilted table is small. The wing captions, field-card labels and
  token labels come out under the smallest text size.
- Desktop: the hand overlaps, so a card's last lines can sit under the next card.
- Phone: beside the longest KO text ("63% chance to OHKO") the HP numbers wrap onto two
  lines; they still fit inside the plate's height.
- Both: "Their hits on you" is not drawn. That direction of the best-hits table needs a
  home (a switch on the KO Board, as in your version).
- Both: the bottom navigation bar is hidden in the full-page phone pictures (it is shown in
  the two first-screen pictures).

8. NOT DRAWN OR NOT CHECKED — please check
- iPad, upright and on its side: not drawn. Sim wants it after this is built.
- Four set moves (the hand then holds three cards plus "Other moves") and one set move.
- A guaranteed OHKO, a tiny hit, a move that is not calculated (Counter, Endeavor), the
  "Pick a damaging move" state, and very long KO text (the Focus Sash wording).
- Rain, sand, snow and each terrain as a lit field card (only Sun is drawn).
- An empty team (no "yours" on the KO Board) and fewer than 30 threats.
- Phones smaller than 390 x 844 (the first-screen fit has only 7 to spare), a 1366 x 768
  laptop, and keyboard and screen-reader order.
- Scrolling smoothness on desktop: the tilt, the glows and the dimmed KO tiles are effects
  Sim has had trouble with on her Windows desktop (her rule: no filtered sprites on their
  own layers).

9. KNOWN CONFLICTS WITH YOUR RULES (resolve your way)
- New colours needed: lit weather cards, move-card faces, the KO tint.
- Off the type scale: the result number (84 desktop, 44 phone), the outlined power numbers,
  the "+".
- Off the 4px grid: most table positions, the tilt, the fan and card angles.
- The desktop mock-up uses a 3D tilt, drop-shadow glows and a grey filter on knocked-out
  tiles.

10. WORDING AND INFORMATION THAT DIFFER FROM TODAY (Sim has seen these; confirm with her)
- "No weather" / "No terrain" read "None" under their labels.
- Each set move shows its own range and KO text (today: names only), plus type, category
  and power.
- The page title is one small line ("Battle · Damage Calc · level 50 doubles" on desktop,
  "Damage Calc · level 50 doubles" on phones), no big heading.
- The KO Board keeps your three counts. Its tiles are picture-only on both sizes (Pokémon,
  who hits it, KO stamp, HP-left bar); the name and move show once a tile is on the table.
- On phones the result's "x–y of z HP" and the effectiveness text are on separate lines.

11. SIM'S STANDING RULES THAT APPLY
- Preview before live; real data only; never remove functionality; ask when unsure.
- Works in light AND dark, on iPhone, iPad and Windows desktop.
- Good use of space, symmetry, no boxes inside boxes, thin soft dividers.
- Fonts do not change. It must not look like a children's site: restrained colour, the
  Pokémon's own colour and the type colours only.
