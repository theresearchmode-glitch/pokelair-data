Everything we save

NastyPlot never asks for your name, email or any contact details, and never reads them from your Claude account

Your teams

Your Claude account

Your current team, saved teams, box, the opponent team you’re testing and your battle log are saved under an anonymous ID (not your name), so they’re there next time. Anyone who can open this page could technically read that saved data, so don’t put anything private in team names.

Usage counts

Anonymous

How many times each page or button was used in a visit, with the date, your screen size group (phone, tablet or desktop), day or night mode and how long the page took to show. If something breaks, the error message is counted too, with anything that looks like an email, phone number or @handle removed. Never what you type, and no first- or last-visit dates. Only NastyPlot’s owner can read these.

Feedback

Only what you send

The message you send from Send Feedback, its kind, the page you came from, phone or desktop and the date (not the time). Email addresses, phone numbers and @handles typed into a message are removed before it’s saved. There’s no field for contact details. Only the owner can read feedback.

One anonymous ID

Never a name or email

The page’s storage only lets each person write their own records, so your teams, usage counts and feedback are each filed under the same anonymous ID that Claude gives this page. It’s a random code, not your name, email or username, and NastyPlot never looks up who an ID belongs to.

On this device only

This browser

Your theme, which sections are open, tab, sort and filter choices, and a copy of your teams stay in this browser’s storage. They never leave your device; clearing your browser data removes them.

Fonts

NastyPlot’s own files

Fonts load from NastyPlot itself, so no font service sees a request from your device.

Nothing else: no cookies, no ads or trackers. Want your saved data or feedback deleted? Email us (Removal & Contact).
