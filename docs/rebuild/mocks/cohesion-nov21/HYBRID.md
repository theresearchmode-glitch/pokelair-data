## Hybrid rule (proposal)
Each page keeps the PC's complete feature list, told with HH's structure:
1. One story, at most 3 plan steps; the hero is the data.
2. Art on every screen: list rows over 6 get a 40px picture; "waiting" states show art (the underdog, faded likely picks), never grey dashed boxes.
3. One pink move per page; pink only for that move and bad verdicts; blue only for taps; "yours" gets its own marker (black chip/underline).
4. At most one link row per step; extra details live one tap away on the item's own page (not a fold).
5. Sentences cap at about 6 boxed words; more options become one "[sun ▾]" word (as the Calc phone does).
6. Phone hero: card on top full width, facts as a 3-across row under it, the pink move full width on one line.
7. Rows of six on phones: 3 x 2 or one row of six small pictures, never 5 + 1; names drop a size before wrapping, never a lone letter.
8. Night: the pink move stays saturated with white text.
9. A 390 overlap/edge check that fails the build (the PC's check passed visible overlaps).
