## Privacy

**Short version: we don't know who you are, and we don't want to.**
NastyPlot has no accounts, no sign-ups, no ads and no trackers.

### What stays on your device
Your teams, your Box, your settings and the games you log are saved **only in your browser**, on your device.
We never see them. Clear your browser's data and they're gone, so use **Export my teams** to keep a copy. [when built]

### What reaches us
- **Feedback you choose to send.** Just your message, the page you were on, phone or computer, and the date.
  Before it's saved, we remove anything that looks like an email address, a phone number or a @handle. We never ask who you are.
- **Page counts.** We count how many times each page is opened each day, as totals only (like "Damage Calc: 412 visits").
  No cookies, no IDs, nothing that tells one visitor from another. (Cloudflare Web Analytics.) [when built]
- **Error reports.** If a page breaks in your browser, we get the error and the page name, so we can fix it. Nothing about you. [when built]

### What we never do
- No accounts, no names, no emails, no passwords.
- No ads, no selling data, no third-party trackers.
- We never ask for your age, your location or your contact details.

### For parents
Lots of Pokémon players are kids. NastyPlot collects no personal information from anyone, so there's nothing about your child
to collect, store or share. Questions? Our email is on the About page (tap "Show email").

### Where our numbers come from
Tournament data from **Limitless** (online tournaments), **official Pokémon events**, the **Showdown ladder**, Smogon's monthly
usage stats and the in-game ranked ladder. Player names are replaced with stand-ins; we only show teams and results.

### Not affiliated
NastyPlot is an unofficial, fan-made tool, not affiliated with Nintendo, The Pokémon Company, Game Freak or Creatures.
Pokémon names and images belong to their owners.
