## 4. Why three pages stay over 3.0 at 1280 (my reading of the numbers, not a test)

The cap in screens means 2,700 px at 1440x900 but only 2,400 px at 1280x800. Events (2642 px at 1440), Speed Tiers (2583) and Test Run
(2604) are nearly as long at 1440 as at 1280; they pass at 1440 because the window is taller. Their long parts already sit two-up or
beside a panel (Speed's field rows from 1280, Test Run's piles beside the side panel, Events' tickets three across). Getting under
2,400 px would need one of: hiding rows behind "Show more" (a fold, not allowed), fewer tickets on Events' wall (removed content), or a
new layout for the piece itself (Test Run rows on one line, Speed Tiers' bands as one-line rows, Events tickets four across with the
date on top). Those are design decisions for HH/Sim, so they are listed as options, not built.
