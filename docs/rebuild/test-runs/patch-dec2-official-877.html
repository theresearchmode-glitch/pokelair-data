Wednesday, December 2, 2026 Battle Balance Adjustments
Thank you very much for your continued support of Pokémon Champions.

The following adjustments to battle balance are planned to be implemented during scheduled maintenance on Wednesday, December 2, 2026.

• The following Pokémon will become able to use the moves listed here.

Psych Up:
Alakazam, Starmie, Mr. Mime, Mawile, Altaria, Castform, Absol, Roserade, Weavile, Rotom, Rotom (Heat Rotom), Rotom (Wash Rotom), Rotom (Frost Rotom), Rotom (Fan Rotom), Rotom (Mow Rotom), Liepard, Musharna, Audino, Cofagrigus, Slurpuff, Drampa, Annihilape

Slack Off:
Emboar

Roost:
Beedrill, Hydreigon

Tailwind:
Emolga

Wonder Room:
Umbreon

Play Rough:
Vaporeon, Jolteon, Flareon, Espeon, Umbreon, Leafeon, Glaceon

• Adjustments will be made to the following moves.
Screech: 85% → 100% accuracy
Rock Slide: 90% → 95% accuracy, 30% → 20% chance to make targets flinch
Steel Wing: 90% → 100% accuracy
Metal Sound: 85% → 100% accuracy
Frost Breath: 90% → 100% accuracy

• Adjustments will be made to the following Abilities.
Flash Fire:
When the Ability Flash Fire is activated, the Pokémon now gains the Fire Boost status. The Fire Boost status will persist even if the Pokémon Mega Evolves.

Stalwart:
The Pokémon ignores the effects of Abilities and moves that draw in moves.
↓
The Pokémon ignores the effects of Abilities and moves that draw in moves.
When using its moves, the Pokémon ignores the effects of targets' substitutes.


Note: The balance adjustments listed above will be applied to battles in all regulations.

We hope you continue to enjoy Pokémon Champions.
