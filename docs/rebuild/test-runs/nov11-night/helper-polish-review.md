## Findings (most visible first)
1. **Team, 768-1179 (seen at 768 and 1023, light): the right half is empty.** The work grid puts Matchups in the left column and only Team Checks plus Your Games on the right. The right column ends about 600px (1023) to 1000px (768) above the end of Matchups, leaving a large empty block. Source: pages.css:52 and :458, `.tm-work` / `.tm-work:has(.tm-yg)` areas `"mu ck" "mu yg"`. Fix: move Suggestions into the right column under Your Games (`"mu ck" "mu yg" "mu sg"`). Alternatively, give Matchups the full row and show its Unfavoured and Favoured lists side by side. Seen in screenshot.
2. **Dark mode: the selected option on every slider is nearly invisible** (Stand-outs, Box + catch, Megas "2", Swap "No"; seen at 375 and 1440). The thumb gradient (#3E3D56 to #2C2B3D) sits on --wash #201F2D, and both labels are light. Chips get a --plot outline at night (components.css:21), but `.np-slide .thumb` (components.css:76) does not. Fix: give the night thumb the --main blue gradient with --on-main text, like the primary button. That keeps it bold. A minimum fix is the same 1.5px --plot inset outline the chips use. Seen in screenshot.
3. **Outline buttons in two heights, side by side** (seen at 1440 and 1023 on team, and at 768 on team-empty). "Share" is 44px next to the 52px "Update Saved Team". "Add a Pokémon" is 52px because its flex row stretches it, but "Add Flapple to My Team" is 44px. This breaks the rule of one size per kind of control. Source: components.css:10 `.np-btn2{min-height:var(--tap)}`, whose height depends on the row's alignment. Fix: one height for `.np-btn2` everywhere (52px to match `.np-btn`), or set `align-items:stretch` in `.tm-acts` and `.tm-e-lead .row`. Seen in screenshot.
4. **Team hero at 900-1179 (seen at 1023): a large empty area at top right.** The right column holds only the action buttons, pushed to the bottom right. "Saved Teams 1" and "More" float under them, and about 550 by 200px above is empty. Source: pages.css:143, areas `"lock acts" "info acts" "six six"`, `.tm-acts{align-self:end;justify-content:end}`. Fix: put the "Teams scoring like this…" and rank lines in the right column with the buttons beneath them, keeping the 93 large on the left. Seen in screenshot.
