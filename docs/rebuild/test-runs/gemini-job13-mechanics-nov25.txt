1.4 Stat formula & rounding
  Champions:
    HP = Base + StatPoints + 75
    Other = floor((Base + StatPoints + 20) * Alignment)
    where Alignment is 1.1 (+), 0.9 (-), or 1.0 (neutral).
  SV: floor((2*Base + IV + floor(EV/4)) * 50 / 100) + 60 (HP) or floor((floor((2*Base + IV + floor(EV/4)) * 50 / 100) + 5) * Nature). With IV=31 and EV->SP, algebraically identical.
  Source: https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Stat_point (T3, 2026-11-24)
    Quote: "HP = Base + StatPoints + 75; Other Stat = floor((Base + StatPoints + 20) * Alignment) where Alignment is 0.9 if the Pokémon's stat alignment lowers that stat, 1.1 if it raises that stat, and 1 otherwise."
  Our code: app/src/app/39-damage-calc-speed.js:12:
    `function calcStat(p,stat,sp,mul){const b=p.st[STAT_IX[stat]];if(stat==='HP')return b+75+sp;return Math.floor((b+20+sp)*mul)}`
  Status: MATCH

TOP 5 PLACES OUR DATA IS MOST LIKELY WRONG THAT NOBODY CHECKED YET:
1. **`data.js` `LEARN` (349 hand-built learnsets from October):** Because `LEARN` was hand-built in October with no generator, any move removed or restricted in Champions (like Incineroar losing Knock Off or Politoed losing Pound) or form-specific learnset difference (like `Meowstic-F`, regional forms, or Mega forms inheriting base moves) is prime territory for silent `WRONG-LEGAL` errors. Diffing `LEARN` programmatically against Showdown's `data/mods/champions/learnsets.ts` will likely catch multiple illegal or missing moves.
2. **`ABILITY_MODS` & `WEATHER_OF`/`TERRAIN_OF` in the unbundled core file:** `39-damage-calc-speed.js` relies on external `ABILITY_MODS` for Ground immunity (`Eelevate`), `WEATHER_OF`, and `TERRAIN_OF`. If `Eelevate: {Ground: 0}` was missed in `ABILITY_MODS` the same way it was missed in `allyHit()` (`39-damage-calc-speed.js:138`), then Mega Eelektross takes full Ground damage in `damage()` AND on the type chart!
3. **`Palafin` Zero vs Hero Form stats across Calc, Speed Tiers, and Strength Score:** `ROSTER` stores Palafin's Zero Form (`[100,70,72,53,62,100]`). If the boot script doesn't swap it to Hero Form (`[100,160,97,106,87,100]`) cleanly everywhere, every Palafin calc and threat matchup is off by 90 base Attack.
4. **Curly apostrophes (`Farfetch’d` / `Sirfetch’d` using U+2019) vs Showdown ASCII pastes (`Farfetch'd` / `Sirfetch'd`):** Any Pokepaste import, team link, or external usage sync that doesn't normalize U+2019 <-> U+0027 will fail `byName.has()` and silently drop Farfetch'd or Sirfetch'd into `gone`.
5. **Pre-patch `info-text.js` & `infosheet.js` vs Champions edge-case moves:** Beyond the Dec 2 patch, Champions changed `Salt Cure` passive chip (halved to 1/16 and 1/8), `Rage Fist` (resets on switch-out), `Fake Out`/`First Impression` (Encore forces Struggle), `Flame Body` (activates even when fainting), and `Eject Button` (no longer blocks U-turn/Volt Switch/Flip Turn). Any hand-written copy in `infosheet.js` or `OVR` in `gen-info-text.mjs` that still quotes SV behavior for those 5 mechanics will be wrong on the site.
